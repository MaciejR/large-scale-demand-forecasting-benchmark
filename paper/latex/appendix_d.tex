\section{Supplemental Heuristic-Weight Diagnostics}\label{app:sensitivity}

The Source~A sensitivity variants use model-level log-ratios,
\texttt{metafor::rma.mv} with REML, \texttt{test = "t"},
\texttt{dfs = "contain"}, and the random-effects structure described in
\S\ref{subsec:metaspec}.  Negative estimates indicate lower error/loss for
the foundation model.  Generated output files are under
\path{analysis/figures/}.  Because $v_i$ is a workload-count proxy rather
than a validated sampling variance, the intervals, $p$ values, and $Q$
statistics below are diagnostic output, not confirmatory meta-analysis.

\subsection{Interpretation}

The sign is stable across sensitivity variants, but conservative
suite-level containment intervals cross zero.  This remains the key
methodological constraint: the benchmark rows provide many task-level
comparisons, but they do not provide many independent source clusters.  The
Source~B matched-panel rerun is useful local paired evidence, but it is not
pooled into these Source~A diagnostic variants.

Task-clustered CR2 sensitivity fits are more optimistic because they treat
the 24 benchmark tasks as clusters.  These are retained as software
diagnostics only; they do not replace the missing paired sampling covariance.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{Bucket-Level Variant ($k = 43$)}{Bucket-Level Variant (k = 43)}}

For comparison, a coarser bucket-level aggregation
(mean FM vs mean ML\_TREE per dataset--horizon--metric cell) yields
$k = 43$ and $\hat{\ell}=-0.180$ (95\% CI $[-0.313,-0.047]$,
$p=0.015$).  This variant is retained only as a historical sensitivity check;
it averages away within-task model heterogeneity and is not the primary
estimand.
