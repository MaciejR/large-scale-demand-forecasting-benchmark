\section{Structured-Search Reporting Checklist}\label{app:prisma}

The following table maps PRISMA 2020 reporting items \citep{page2021prisma}
to the closest section of this benchmark study.  The mapping is used as a
transparency checklist for a single-author structured search, not as a claim
of full systematic-review compliance.
