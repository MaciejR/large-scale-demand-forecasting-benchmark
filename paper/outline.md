# Paper Outline

## Title
**Demand Forecasting for Short-Term, Large-Scale Retail: A Systematic Review and Benchmark**

## Abstract
This paper presents a systematic review and large-scale benchmark of demand forecasting methods for short-term horizons in retail and supply chain settings. We compare statistical, machine learning, deep learning, transformer-based, and foundation models under a unified experimental protocol across multiple public datasets. The study emphasizes not only forecast accuracy but also computational cost, stability, and scalability.
