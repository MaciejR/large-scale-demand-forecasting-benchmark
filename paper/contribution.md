# Contributions and Positioning

This work makes the following contributions:

1. A systematic review of demand forecasting methods with a focus on short-term, large-scale retail problems.
2. A unified and reproducible benchmark comparing statistical, machine learning, deep learning, transformer-based, and foundation models.
3. An empirical analysis of forecast accuracy, stability, and computational cost across multiple datasets.
4. Practical recommendations for practitioners and researchers working on large-scale demand forecasting.

## Positioning
The paper targets applied forecasting and operations research venues such as the International Journal of Forecasting (IJF), European Journal of Operational Research (EJOR), and data-driven applied machine learning conferences.
